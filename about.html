Who We Are

M.P. Industries is a Kichha-based manufacturer of polyethylene films, bags and BOPP self-adhesive tapes. Founded with a commitment to quality and fair pricing, we have grown into a trusted packaging partner for factories, traders, dairy units and retailers across Uttarakhand and Uttar Pradesh.

Our manufacturing unit at Khasra No. 392, Village-Devariya runs modern blown-film extrusion lines capable of producing LLDPE, HDPE and LDPE films across a wide range of widths and gauges. We also undertake flexographic printing for branded bags and custom packing jobs.

We believe that good packaging is not just about protection — it's a statement of quality for your brand. That's why we are "Quality Assured".
